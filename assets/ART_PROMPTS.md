# 아트 콘셉트 생성 프롬프트

기본 제공 이미지 생성 도구로 생성했다. 캐릭터 시트는 투명 배경, 경기장은 불투명 배경을 요청했다.

## 캐릭터 시트 최종본

> Create ONE usable transparent sprite sheet for a small portrait mobile soccer game, with exactly 16 complete boy soccer characters in a rigid 4 columns × 4 rows layout. PRIORITY: sprite extraction geometry. Each figure and its football must fit entirely within its own invisible cell, centered and drawn at only about 70% of cell height, with at least 25 pixels of perfectly transparent space at the TOP, BOTTOM, LEFT and RIGHT of EVERY cell. All 16 cells have the same dimensions and consistent character scale. There must be clear empty gutters between each row, including between rows 3 and 4; NO limbs, balls, hair, shadows or outlines may touch a cell boundary. Columns represent four stable identities: cheerful average boy with short BLACK sports hair; agile boy with swept BLACK hair; sturdier confident boy with short BLACK hair; goalkeeper with BLACK hair and prominent gloves. Rows: idle ready, running, passing kick with ball, powerful shooting with ball. Front three-quarter view from a slightly elevated game camera, full body, chibi large head and short compact body, friendly expressive Korean children's mobile game animation style, clean medium outlines and soft 3D cel shading, no exaggerated anime. Invented vivid-red Korea-inspired uniform, navy shorts, white trim, no emblems; yellow goalkeeper shirt and navy shorts. Transparent clean alpha, no background or ground, no ambient red glow, no red splatters, no detached colored strokes, no motion effects, no typography, logos, numbers, brand marks or watermark. Preserve crisp silhouettes that remain readable at 48-pixel screen height.

## 경기장 콘셉트 최종본

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready background concept for a portrait mobile HTML5 children's soccer game. One vertical 9:16-ish full-frame background, illustrated 2D mobile game quality, no characters and no ball. View from an elevated game camera at the near end of a long football pitch, looking toward the distant opposite goal and cheerful stadium stands. Strong but natural field perspective; the playable grass occupies most of the frame, with a broad clean center corridor for tiny players and readable white ball, distant goal and near goal/net both coherent. Existing game's simple bright-green pitch with modest spectator stand should feel one generation more polished, still clearly the same child-friendly game. Mid-saturation natural greens, subtle alternating mowing stripes and restrained grass texture, crisp white center line, center circle and penalty-box markings, dimensional clean white goal frames and net. Side edges gently darkened for focus; soft sunny daytime light without hard shadows. Top 12% reserved as a visually calm low-detail region for a HUD; bottom 15% kept low-detail for touch controls. Simple cute crowd dots and silhouettes without faces, colorful banners, geometric ad boards with small abstract star/ball/lightning/trophy shapes only. Clear depth separation suitable for later extraction into six layers: far sky/stadium, crowd stand, ad boards, grass, field markings, goals/net. Sports-game polished Korean children's mobile animation style, clean controlled shapes and subtle 3D-like cel shading, vibrant but not fluorescent, no photorealism. IMPORTANT: preserve high contrast for red, blue, yellow and white kits and turquoise/orange/white gesture lines. Absolutely NO footballers, people on pitch, ball, controls, scoreboards, UI buttons, text, letters, logos, real clubs, brands, watermarks, confusing painted figures, or oversized stadium details. Avoid visual clutter on the pitch.

## GPT 이미지 생성 선수 마스터 v1

- 방식: Codex 내장 `image_gen` 생성. 기준 Idle 생성 후 같은 대화의 직전 캐릭터 이미지를 매번 참조해 포즈별로 생성했다.
- 공통 핵심 프롬프트: “One original fictional Korean boy football protagonist around 4-head-tall chibi-athletic proportions. Friendly confident face, short dark sporty hair, red football shirt with clean white and restrained navy trim, navy shorts, red socks, dark boots. Premium polished 2D cel-shaded family sports game art with consistent clean outlines, restrained highlights, slightly elevated mobile game three-quarter view. Centered full body, every limb and boot visible, transparent alpha background, no glow or backdrop, no ball, no text, no logos, no watermark.”
- 포즈별 추가 지시: `idle` balanced ready stance; `run` compact running stride with opposite arm/leg; `pass` controlled inside-foot pass; `shot` powerful forward kick; `tackle` low safe interception lunge. For `goalkeeper-idle`, keep the same boy and art style but use yellow/teal goalkeeper kit and padded gloves in a ready stance. For `goalkeeper-save`, preserve that keeper identity and kit in a horizontal side-dive save.
- 저장 위치: 원본 PNG는 `players/source/korea-*-v1.png`, 128×136px lossless WebP는 `players/korea/ai-v1/`, 메뉴용 최적화 파일은 `ui/home-player-v1.webp`.
- 메모: 여러 포즈의 인물 일관성을 모델이 완벽히 보장한다고 보지 않는다. 게임 크기 접촉 시트와 Playwright 캡처를 검토하고, 발 기준선 및 공통 캔버스를 후처리로 맞춘다. 생성된 프레임은 유니폼 색이 포함된 대한민국 proof-of-concept다.
